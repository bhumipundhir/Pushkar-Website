About Pushkar

A Sacred Legacy

Nestled amidst the sacred hills of Rajasthan, Pushkar is a timeless destination of faith, colors, and spirituality. Home to 52 holy ghats and more than 500 historic temples, it is where history comes alive through vibrant folk tradition and natural beauty.
