Rich Heritage of Temples in Pushkar

Shri Brahma Mandir

The Brahma Temple in Pushkar, one of the few dedicated to Lord Brahma in India, is a must-visit. With its striking red spire and white marble structure.

Shree Savitri Mata Mandir

Perched on Ratnagiri Hill, this temple dedicated to Goddess Savitri offers panoramic views of Pushkar and the surrounding desert.

Rangji Temple

A unique temple blending South Indian and Rajasthani architectural styles, dedicated to Lord Rangji (a form of Vishnu).

Varaha Temple

An ancient temple dedicated to Lord Vishnu's boar incarnation. It features beautiful carvings and is one of Pushkar's oldest and most architecturally significant temples.
